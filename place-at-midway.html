Executive summary

Place at Midway is a 200-unit, 1989-vintage workforce community in Douglas County, where Post currently owns 1,229 units (Crestmark, Sweetwater Creek, Villas at West Ridge, Valera Riverside) under the Douglas County Housing Authority structure. In-place rents sit $550 to $760 per month below the 80% AMI maximum on every floor plan, so a conversion requires no rent concession. The seller is an institutional fund completing the disposition of a 2020 three-property package and has an existing relationship with Post. Recommendation: execute the confidentiality agreement, obtain the offering memorandum and rent roll, and bring to the Monday pipeline meeting.

Fit with Post strategy

  • Existing partner and documents. Douglas County Housing Authority has closed Crestmark (334), Sweetwater Creek and Villas at West Ridge (470) and Valera Riverside (425) with Post. No new jurisdiction, structure or counsel required.
  • Operating platform in place. TAM Residential manages all three neighboring assets; regional oversight, maintenance and vendors are shared.
  • Within the acquisition profile. 200 units, 1989 vintage, Class B. 2025 to 2026 closings ranged from 95 to 696 units with a median vintage of 1985.
  • Closest internal comparable. Park on Sweetwater: 304 units, 1986, Gwinnett County HA, acquired 2024 at approximately $157K per unit.

Seller and transaction history

  • FCP acquired the asset in March 2020 within a 652-unit, $71.7M package (Place at Midway; Stewart's Mill, Douglasville; Forest Glen, Austell), assuming Fannie Mae debt. Broker on that sale: Nathan Swenson, Cushman & Wakefield, who is listing the asset today.
  • FCP sold Stewart's Mill to Viking Capital in December 2023 for $28.2M. This listing completes the package disposition; the seller is on a fund timeline.
  • FCP is an existing Post relationship: equity partner on Park on Sweetwater and Hendry, and client of Post's Georgia housing-authority advisory work in 2023 (Ashford at Spring Lake, Asher at Sugarloaf, Wyndcliff, Westwood Glen). A principal-to-principal conversation is available.

Key risks and diligence items

  • Partner capacity. Douglas County HA has paused new Post deals for 2026 after four closings. Position as the first 2027 transaction (the April 1 deadline already requires a Q1 close), confirm Crestmark certifications are current before the ask, and be ready to offer a higher PILOT. Fallbacks for counsel: alternate authority with jurisdiction, or a 501c3 bond execution.
  • Submarket demand. Crestmark is 87% occupied with staffing issues. Confirm with asset management whether weakness is property-specific or submarket-wide before adding exposure.
  • Concentration. Acquisition would bring Post to 1,429 units in one Douglas County submarket; Valera (2022) adds recent supply.
  • Physical condition. 1989 construction: roofs, plumbing, siding, HVAC. Hillcrest or 1st Call site inspection prior to pricing.
  • Exemption timing. Georgia requires application by April 1; a close after that date defers the exemption a full year. Target a first-quarter 2027 closing.
  • Renovation narrative. 73% classic units will be marketed as value-add. The Post return does not depend on it; treat as upside.
