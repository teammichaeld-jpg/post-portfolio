In one sentence: Avenues is churning. Half the residents leave at renewal, 46 homes turned last month, every turn goes to one contractor at full price, and the new leases sign 7% below the old ones because 800 brand-new units up the road are giving six to eight weeks free. The two income programs the deal counted on, washer-dryer rent and income certification, are both stalled, and the certification deadline that protects the tax exemption is six months out. Same AM, same RPM, same turn vendor as Crestmark and Valera. This is a team problem showing up on a third property.

Why
About 800 brand-new units are leasing up within three miles: Rue 108 (229 units, under a mile, pre-leasing), Solis Kennesaw (239, six weeks free), The Parker (330, eight weeks free). Avenues' one bedrooms are $130 to $450 cheaper on net rent and still lose the renter who can stretch.
The homes typed 60% and 80% AMI re-rent at 7.6% and 7.9% below the prior resident, versus 4.4% on market units. The rent caps cost about three points on those units. That is the price of the structure, but which units carry the 60% tag is a choice.
Traffic is strong, 65 to 131 prospects a week. The miss is at the lease desk and the turn board. Leasing was offered double commission to hit 19 by Sep 23 and signed 6.
Online rating is 2.9 out of 5 on 165 reviews. The three new competitors are all above 4.
