1. Cypress Station Square, Houston

Marcus & Millichap · 1986, renovated 2019 · 12.19 acres · NOI $1.98M · 275,000 cars a day at I-45 and FM 1960

  • Why Ethan. Same shape as NASA Plaza with better in-place income: 9.25% going in, in-line rents at $12 to $18 NNN, 9% vacant, 850 feet of frontage and pad sites the broker says are leasable or developable. Day-one cash pays 12% on equity.
  • Plan. Lease 12,000 SF at $14, push bumps, carve one or two pads. Stabilized return on cost 9.7%. Exit at 9.0% in year 5.
  • Risks. FM 1960 corridor has slipped; tenants are needs-based, not credit (Workforce Solutions, Milan cosmetology school). Underwrite the school and DaVita terms first. 1986 roofs and parking.
  • Next. OM and rent roll from Marcus & Millichap Houston; ask why 9.25% and how long listed.

2. Fayetteville Commons, NC

Marcus & Millichap · listed June 30, 2026 · 2016 build · 12.09 acres · NOI $1.50M · shadow-anchored by Target and Home Depot

  • Why Ethan. Chesterfield again, newer: two national anchors at $10.30 NNN since 2015, Burlington is the top store in North Carolina, shop rents in the mid-$20s to low-$30s, 1.4M visits a year, no junior-box vacancy in the corridor.
  • Plan. Burlington wants to shrink to 36,348 SF and pay for the split at the same total rent. That frees 13,000 SF the leasing agent prices at $200K a year. Plus two small vacancies. NOI to $1.83M by year 3, return on cost 8.0%.
  • Risks. Two tenants are 89% of the rent. Fort Liberty town. $169/SF is fair, not cheap; the IRR needs the Burlington split to happen.
  • Next. OM, Burlington correspondence on the downsize, Placer data. Bid $20.5M.

How the numbers were run

  • Price plus 4% for closing and working capital, plus a leasing and capital reserve per deal ($200K to $900K).
  • Debt at 62% of total cost, 6.25% fixed, 24 months interest-only, then 30-year amortization (CB Square's SouthState terms).
  • NOI from the listing; rent bumps 2% a year; vacancy leased at the broker's quoted rents over 24 to 36 months.
  • Five-year hold, sold at the going-in cap plus 25 basis points, 2% sale cost. Project level, before PARE fees and promote.
  • Bids are the price at which the deal clears 16% IRR and PARE's 8% preferred return in year one.

Where the list came from

  • LoopNet shopping-center listings in seven states, sorted by price, 227 in Florida alone, 80 in Georgia, 336 in Texas, 115 in Arizona.
  • Kept multi-tenant open-air centers between $8M and $25M with a going-in cap at or above 7%, or a basis under $100/SF.
  • Dropped single-tenant, condo, office, redevelopment and sub-$8M listings, and anything priced below a 6.5% cap.
  • Listing dates matter: Centennial Place (12 months), Island Plaza (35 months) and Point West (18 months) are negotiable; Gilbert and Union Hills are three weeks old.
