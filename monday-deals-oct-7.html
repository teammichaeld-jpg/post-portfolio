What to do this week

Before Monday 1:30 Pacific
  • Place at Midway: email Cushman Atlanta for the OM and rent roll. Send Lash the one-line pullback math. This is the one to bring to the pipeline meeting.
  • Berkadia Atlanta: one call for Stoneview, Sedgefield and Manchester Ridge. Ask vintage, guidance and whether offers have a date.

How to present it
  • Lead with county, units, vintage, offers date, and the pullback read. Nothing else until asked.
  • Name the housing partner that already exists in that county. That is the difference between a 90-day close and a 9-month one.
  • Say which existing Post property it looks like. Rosemont, Crestmark, Avenues, Lakes and Revive. The team prices by comparison.
  • Do not pitch. Hand the associate a deal they can take credit for. The second time it happens, it becomes your job.
