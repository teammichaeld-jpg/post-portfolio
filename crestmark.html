Why we are paying so much more
The budget assumed our own four techs paint and patch. That is why contract painting was budgeted at $3K for the year. Instead, every turn goes to a contractor (YJY Group or Trilogy) at contractor prices. About two-thirds of the overage.
Every unit gets the full package. Paint, sheetrock, tub resurface, counter resurface, whether it needs it or not. Adds $1,000 to $1,500 per unit.
More units are turning. Renewals fell from 73% to 57%, so about 22 units turn a month instead of 15.
Wrong people doing the work, doing more than needed, on more units than planned. Last three months $25K a month vs $14.5K before. And departing residents are billed for 14% of the damage (problem 4).
